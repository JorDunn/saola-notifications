## [0.2.0](https://github.com/JorDunn/saola-notifications/compare/saola-notifications-v0.1.1...saola-notifications-v0.2.0) - 2026-09-07

### Added

- move the saola-theme pin to v0.15.0 and close the two follow-up gaps
- move the saola-theme pin to v0.14.0 and remove the nine theme workarounds
- serve live control properties with PropertiesChanged (stage 9)
- bridge saola-capture signals and add auto-DND (stage 8)
- add the notification centre surface (stage 7)
- show action pills and emit ActionInvoked (stage 6)
- boot the layershell daemon and show toast cards (stage 5)
- add the pure notification store and hint parser (stage 4)
- serve the notification and control D-Bus interfaces (stage 3)
- load notifications.toml and reload it on change (stage 2)

### Fixed

- use as_chunks so clippy 1.98 passes in CI
- enable the wgpu and tiny-skia renderers (stage 10)
